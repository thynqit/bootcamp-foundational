# Assignments – Day 21: Business Requirement Document (BRD)

> Part of the Thynqit Labs – Foundational Engineering Bootcamp

---

# 📌 Overview

In this assignment, learners will think like:

- business analysts
- product teams
- engineering leaders

before implementation begins.

The goal is to understand how:

```plaintext
business problems
    ↓
requirements
    ↓
engineering planning
```

connect together.

Learners will analyze a real-world platform, identify business problems, and create a structured Business Requirement Document (BRD).

---

# 🎯 Assignment Objectives

By completing this assignment, learners should be able to:

- analyze real-world digital platforms
- identify business and operational problems
- define business goals clearly
- create structured BRDs
- define scope boundaries
- identify functional and non-functional requirements
- connect business requirements to engineering decisions

# 📄 Part 3 – Create a BRD

Using:

```plaintext
templates/business-requirements/brd-markdown.md
templates/business-requirements/brd-document.docx
templates/business-requirements/brd-document.pdf
```

create a Business Requirement Document for your selected platform.

Your BRD should include:

- Executive Summary
- Business Problem Statement
- Business Objectives
- Stakeholders
- Scope Definition
- Functional Requirements
- Non-Functional Requirements
- Risks
- Dependencies
- Success Metrics

# 🧠 Engineering Insight

Good engineering begins long before coding starts.

Strong systems are usually built on:

- clear business understanding
- realistic requirements
- well-defined scope
- stakeholder alignment

The quality of engineering decisions often depends on the quality of requirements defined at the beginning.
