# Resources – Day 21: Business Requirement Document (BRD)

> Part of the Thynqit Labs – Foundational Engineering Bootcamp

---

## 📌 Overview

These resources help learners understand:

- what Business Requirement Documents (BRDs) are
- why requirement clarity matters
- how business requirements influence engineering decisions
- how organizations define scope, goals, and success metrics

This module focuses on the connection between:

```plaintext
Business Problems → Product Planning → Engineering Execution
```

# 🧠 Key Takeaway

Strong engineering starts long before coding begins.

Clear business requirements help teams:

- align expectations
- reduce ambiguity
- improve delivery quality
- avoid expensive rework

Understanding requirements is a critical engineering skill.
