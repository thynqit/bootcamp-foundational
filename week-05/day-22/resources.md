# Resources – Day 22: Product Requirement Document (PRD)

> Part of the Thynqit Labs – Foundational Engineering Bootcamp

---

# 📌 Overview

These resources help learners understand:

- what Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) are
- how product teams define functionality
- how user journeys influence product behavior
- how product requirements connect business goals with engineering implementation

This module focuses on the connection between:

```plaintext
Business Goals
    ↓
Product Features
    ↓
Engineering Planning
```

# 📌 Suggested Learning Flow

1. Understand what PRDs are.
2. Learn how product requirements differ from business requirements.
3. Study user journeys and workflow thinking.
4. Understand functional and non-functional requirements.
5. Explore product documentation tools.
6. Review the running example in `example.md`.

---

# 🧠 Key Takeaway

Strong products are built through:

- clear requirement definition
- understanding user problems
- aligning product and engineering teams
- defining measurable outcomes

PRDs help teams transform business goals into usable product experiences.
