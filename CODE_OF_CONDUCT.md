# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At Thynqit Labs, we are committed to fostering an open, respectful, and inclusive community.

This repository is a space for learning, collaboration, and improving engineering practices. We expect all participants to engage constructively and professionally.

---

## Expected Behavior

All contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in communication
- Provide constructive feedback
- Be open to different perspectives and ideas
- Focus on improving the quality of content and discussions
- Help create a positive learning environment

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Spamming or irrelevant promotions
- Disrespectful or unprofessional communication
- Any conduct that negatively impacts the community

---

## Contribution Guidelines

When contributing:

- Ensure your contributions improve clarity, quality, or accuracy
- Keep discussions relevant and professional
- Provide context when suggesting changes
- Follow the structure and intent of the repository

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers of this repository have the right to:

- Remove or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct
- Restrict participation if behavior is deemed inappropriate

---

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please raise an issue or contact the maintainers.

---

## Final Note

This repository reflects how we think about engineering — with clarity, respect, and strong fundamentals.

Let’s build that together.
