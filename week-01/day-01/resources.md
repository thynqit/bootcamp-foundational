# Resources – Day 1: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

> Part of the Thynqit Labs – Foundational Engineering Bootcamp

---

## 📌 Overview

These resources focus on **cybersecurity awareness for engineers and employees**.

The goal is to understand:

- common cyber threats targeting organizations
- how attackers manipulate employees
- how everyday actions can expose sensitive data
- how engineers should behave responsibly when handling company systems and data

This module focuses on **security awareness**, not secure application development.

## 📌 Notes

- Most successful cyber attacks exploit **human mistakes rather than technical vulnerabilities**.
- Phishing and credential theft remain the most common entry points into enterprise systems.
- Security awareness is one of the most important responsibilities for every employee, including engineers.
