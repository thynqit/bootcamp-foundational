# Assignments – Day 2: Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)

> Part of the Thynqit Labs – Foundational Engineering Bootcamp

---

## 📌 Overview

Apply the concepts learned in this module to understand how structured development processes help engineering teams build reliable software systems.

These exercises focus on:

- understanding SDLC phases
- comparing development models
- analyzing real engineering scenarios
- reflecting on process discipline in software projects

---

## 🧠 Conceptual Questions

Answer the following in your own words:

1. What is SDLC and why does it exist?
2. List and explain the typical phases of SDLC.
3. What is the primary difference between Waterfall and Agile?
4. What does “iterative development” mean?
5. Why is testing not just a final phase in modern development?

Avoid copying definitions. Focus on explanation.

---

## 🔍 Comparative Thinking

Create a comparison table between **Waterfall** and **Agile** including:

- Requirement flexibility
- Feedback cycle
- Risk management
- Documentation level
- Suitable project types
- Failure risk

After creating the table, answer:

- In what situations would Waterfall be more appropriate?
- In what situations would Agile be more effective?

---

## 🧩 Scenario-Based Thinking

### Scenario 1 – Regulated System

You are building a **hospital management system** where requirements are clearly defined and heavily regulated.

- Which SDLC model would you choose?
- Why?
- What risks must be considered?

---

### Scenario 2 – Startup Product

You are building a **startup product** where features change frequently based on user feedback.

- Which model would you use?
- Why?
- What challenges may arise?

---

### Scenario 3 – Failure Analysis

A team starts development without clearly defining requirements.  
Midway through the project, major changes are requested.

- What went wrong?
- Which SDLC principle was ignored?
- How could this have been prevented?

---

## 🛠 Practical Exercises

Draw a simple **SDLC diagram** for a small application of your choice.

Examples:

- Task Manager App
- E-commerce Website
- Blogging Platform

For each phase describe:

- What happens in that phase
- Who is responsible
- What deliverables are produced

You may draw digitally or on paper and summarize in writing.

---

## 🔄 Reflection

Write short answers to the following:

1. Why do large companies formalize SDLC?
2. Can Agile exist without discipline?
3. What risks increase when there is no defined development lifecycle?
4. What part of SDLC do engineers usually underestimate?

---

## 📋 Self-Evaluation Checklist

- [ ] I can explain SDLC without reading notes.
- [ ] I understand when to use Waterfall vs Agile.
- [ ] I can describe SDLC phases in a real project.
- [ ] I reflected on process failures.
- [ ] I understand why process maturity matters.

---

## 🚀 Optional Challenge (Advanced)

Research a real-world software failure or postmortem.

Examples include major outages, failed launches, or engineering incidents.

Identify:

- Was it a process failure?
- Was it a testing failure?
- Was it requirement misalignment?
- How could better SDLC discipline have helped?
