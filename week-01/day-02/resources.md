# Resources – Day 2: Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)

> Part of the Thynqit Labs – Foundational Engineering Bootcamp

---

## 📌 Overview

These resources help learners understand the **Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)** and the different approaches used by engineering teams to build and deliver software.

The goal is to understand:

- why structured development processes exist
- how different SDLC models organize software development
- how modern engineering teams adopt Agile practices

Focus on understanding the **reasoning behind development processes**, not memorizing definitions.

## 📌 Notes

- Different organizations implement SDLC differently.
- Modern engineering teams often combine multiple approaches such as Agile, iterative development, and continuous delivery.
- The goal of SDLC is not bureaucracy but **predictability, collaboration, and quality assurance**.
