## 📌 Suggested Approach

1. Start with Agile Alliance or PMI for conceptual clarity.
2. Watch one structured video explanation.
3. Read a practical blog to see real-world examples.
4. Reflect on how SDLC applies to real engineering teams.

> Focus on understanding the reasoning behind each model, not memorizing definitions.
