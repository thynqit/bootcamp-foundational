# Assignments – Day 1: Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)

> Apply the concepts learned in this module.

---

## 🧠 Part 1 – Conceptual Understanding

Answer the following in your own words:

1. What is SDLC and why does it exist?
2. List and explain the typical phases of SDLC.
3. What is the primary difference between Waterfall and Agile?
4. What does “iterative development” mean?
5. Why is testing not just a final phase in modern development?

Avoid copying definitions. Focus on explanation.

---

## 🔍 Part 2 – Comparative Thinking

Create a comparison table between Waterfall and Agile including:

- Requirement flexibility
- Feedback cycle
- Risk management
- Documentation level
- Suitable project types
- Failure risk

After creating the table, answer:

- In what situations would Waterfall be more appropriate?
- In what situations would Agile be more effective?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Scenario-Based Application

### Scenario 1

You are building a hospital management system where requirements are clearly defined and regulated.

- Which SDLC model would you choose?
- Why?
- What risks must be considered?

---

### Scenario 2

You are building a startup product where features change weekly based on user feedback.

- Which model would you use?
- Why?
- What challenges may arise?

---

### Scenario 3 – Failure Analysis

A team starts development without clearly defining requirements.  
Midway through the project, major changes are requested.

- What went wrong?
- Which SDLC principle was ignored?
- How could this have been prevented?

---

## 🛠 Part 4 – Practical Exercise

Draw a simple SDLC diagram for a small application of your choice.

Example:
- Task Manager App
- E-commerce Website
- Blogging Platform

For each phase, briefly describe:

- What happens in that phase
- Who is responsible
- What deliverables are produced

You may draw digitally or on paper and summarize in writing.

---

## 🔄 Part 5 – Reflection

Write short answers to the following:

1. Why do large companies formalize SDLC?
2. Can Agile exist without discipline?
3. What risks increase when there is no defined development lifecycle?
4. What part of SDLC do engineers usually underestimate?
