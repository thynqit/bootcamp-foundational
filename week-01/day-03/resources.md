# Resources – Day 3: Agile & Scrum Framework

> Part of the Thynqit Labs – Foundational Engineering Bootcamp

---

## 📌 Overview

Curated learning resources to understand **Agile principles** and the **Scrum framework** used by modern engineering teams.

These resources help learners understand:

- why Agile emerged
- how Scrum structures team collaboration
- how iterative delivery improves software development

Focus on understanding the **principles behind Agile**, not just memorizing terminology.

---

## 📖 Official Documentation & Authoritative Sources

These are primary, globally recognized references.

- Agile Manifesto (Official Site)  
  https://agilemanifesto.org/

- The Scrum Guide (Official Scrum Guide – Scrum.org)  
  https://scrumguides.org/docs/scrumguide/v2020/2020-Scrum-Guide-US.pdf

- Scrum.org – What is Scrum?  
  https://www.scrum.org/resources/what-is-scrum

These sources define Agile values and the Scrum framework formally.

## 📌 Suggested Learning Flow

1. Read the Agile Manifesto (short but important).
2. Skim the Scrum Guide for structure and terminology.
3. Watch one structured YouTube explanation.
4. Read a practical blog explaining how Scrum works in real teams.
5. Reflect on how this compares with traditional project management.

> Focus on understanding why Agile exists — not just memorizing roles and ceremonies.
