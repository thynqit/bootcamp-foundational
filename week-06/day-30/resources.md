# 🧠 Key Takeaway

Modern internet-scale systems remain reliable through combinations of:

- observability
- redundancy
- resilience patterns
- monitoring
- automated recovery
- operational maturity

Performance and reliability engineering are continuous operational disciplines—not one-time implementation tasks.
